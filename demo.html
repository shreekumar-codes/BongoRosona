Our Heritage

More Than Food. It's a Royal Experience.

Rooted in the royal kitchens of the Nawabs, Bongo Rosona brings the authentic art of Dum pukht cooking to Durgapur. We meticulously slow-cook our biryani in sealed handis, allowing the fragrant basmati rice, tender meats, and secret blend of roasted spices to meld into a symphony of flavours.

Every dish is a testament to our commitment to tradition, quality, and the profound joy of a truly magnificent meal.
